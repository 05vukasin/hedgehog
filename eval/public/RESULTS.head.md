Reading it: hedgehog keeps 95–98 % of the answer values in clean tables, with the right column header far
more often than a plain pandas read (whose header row is the title on these files), at one sixth to one
eighth of MarkItDown's tokens on MiMoTable and RealHiTBench; the catalog itself holds 30–42 % of the
verbatim answers, the rest is a query away. The weak spots are below.

## Failure classes (hedgehog)

1. **Hierarchical row labels lose their parent level.** Row labels exact 66.7 % against 90.7 % for the leaf:
   a section row such as "Region of ancestry" above "Africa" is not carried into a column.
   `statcan/1004.xlsx` C7: gold `region of ancestry › africa`, row labels `['Africa']`;
   `nsf/102_53_nsf19315-tab001.xlsx` C9: gold `2016 › all industries`, labels `['All industries']`.
   (Part of the gap is HiTab's gold: `statcan/1056.xlsx` N8 gold `negative`, cell "Negative (fair, poor)";
   the lenient rate, 74.1 %, absorbs those.)
2. **Header dehyphenation joins English compounds.** `core/src/normalize/headers.rs` `join_hyphenation`
   turns "French-language workers" into "Frenchlanguage workers" and "Family-sponsored immigrants" into
   "Familysponsored immigrants" (plain ASCII hyphen, no line break). 69 of the 82 HiTab `header_answer`
   misses are this: `statcan/102.xlsx` B4, `statcan/1001.xlsx` E3, `statcan/100.xlsx` B4.
   Reproduce with a 3-line CSV whose header is `French-language workers`.
3. **One-value-column tables are not detected.** `statcan/107.xlsx` (one `percentage` column under
   question groups): 0 tables, all 38 cells go to Notes; `statcan/1011.xlsx` likewise; MiMoTable
   `StockSituation4.xlsx`; RealHiTBench `education-table03.xlsx` (a pivot-table sheet). All 41 HiTab lookups
   that are in no table (11 files), and a quarter to a third of the C misses on the other two datasets.
4. **Values outside every table.** Summary cells above or beside the table stay in Notes: MiMoTable
   `SalespersonMonthlySalesRevenueSummaryAnalysisTable1.xlsx` I3, `CashJournal-IncomeandExpenditureTable3.xlsx`
   H2; RealHiTBench `business-table06.xlsx` T12 ("England", a side block).
5. **One malformed number keeps a whole column as text.** `statcan/1261.xlsx` writes negatives as
   `(11,134` (no closing parenthesis); every year column stays text, so "13,505" is not a number (12 lookups).
6. **Table ranges on Enron-style sheets (TableSense).** F1 67.8 % at IoU 0.5 and 37.9 % at exact ranges;
   VFUSE drops to 27.7 % at IoU 0.8 (tables split or merged with neighbouring blocks).

The pandas baseline's failures are structural: `header=0` takes the title row as the header (column header
exact 0 % on HiTab; `Unnamed: n` columns), StatCan numbers stay strings (11.5 % stored as numbers), and
nothing separates total rows from data (3 % clean operand columns).

## Not measured

Whether an agent finds or computes the answer (needs a model); free-text analysis, charts and edits
(MiMoTable Reasoning / Visualize / Edit, RealHiTBench Data Analysis / Visualization: 350 + 1,325 items);
MiMoTable answers whose atoms are not cells of the file are only checked for clean operand columns, by a
word-overlap heuristic; ToTTo tables of HiTab (no spreadsheet) and 168 StatCan tables whose originals are
not in HiTab's archive; SpreadsheetBench and SheetBench (later, with a model).
