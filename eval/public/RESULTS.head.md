Reading it: hedgehog keeps 98–99.8 % of the answer values in clean tables, with the right column header far
more often than a plain pandas read (whose header row is the title on these files), at one sixth to one
eighth of MarkItDown's tokens on MiMoTable and RealHiTBench; the catalog itself holds 31–42 % of the
verbatim answers, the rest is a query away.

HiTab row labels by the depth of the gold row path: one level 88.8 % exact (= leaf); two levels or more
68.2 % exact (was 49.9 %) against 92.1 % for the leaf. The rest of that gap: three-level outlines (the
`<label>_parent` column holds the immediate parent only), parents not written in the sheet (HiTab's gold adds
them, `canada`, `all articles`), labels HiTab rewrote (`negative` for "Negative (fair, poor)"), and outlines
without indentation.

## Failure classes (hedgehog) and their status

1. **Hierarchical row labels lost their parent level** (fixed in part). Row labels in an indented outline
   get `<label>_parent` (the nearest less-indented label above; cell indent or leading spaces), and a first
   section label written in the header's last row (`Region of ancestry | percent`, `Current $billions`)
   goes to the group column. Row labels exact 66.7 → 77.1 %.
2. **Header dehyphenation joined English compounds** (fixed). "French-language" keeps its hyphen; an
   in-word hyphen is joined only for months, suffix fragments, words the sheet writes joined, or outside
   English. Column header exact 91.2 → 93.7 %. Known residual: BEA "Telecommuni-cations".
3. **One-value-column tables were not detected** (fixed). A lone label over the value column (over section
   labels, or directly over a number when the region has no table otherwise), a sparse row over a wide
   header, and long `label | number` lists. HiTab lookups in no table 41 → 5. Not fixed: RealHiTBench
   `education-table03.xlsx` (pivot rows `Sum of …` read as total rows).
4. **Values outside every table** (fixed for labelled numbers). Numbers with their labels outside the
   tables form a key/value table per sheet (`key`, `value`, `cell`), one catalog line. MiMoTable cell
   95.4 → 98.6 %. Not fixed: RealHiTBench side blocks of labels without numbers (`business-table06.xlsx`
   T12 "England").
5. **One malformed number kept a whole column as text** (fixed). `(11,134` reads as a negative, listed in a
   column warning. Kept as text 12 → 3; four `statcan/1261` lookups now differ in sign from HiTab's answer
   (the question rephrases the negative net liability as a positive net asset).
6. **Table ranges on Enron-style sheets (TableSense).** F1 68.0 % at IoU 0.5 and 38.2 % at exact ranges
   (open).

The pandas baseline's failures are structural: `header=0` takes the title row as the header (column header
exact 0 % on HiTab; `Unnamed: n` columns), StatCan numbers stay strings (11.5 % stored as numbers), and
nothing separates total rows from data (3 % clean operand columns).
