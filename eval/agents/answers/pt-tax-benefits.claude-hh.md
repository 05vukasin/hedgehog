All four answers below come from the IRC and IVA sheets of the 2023 file. The VAT figures add up to the sheet's own total row (IVA, row 4324), and every row has its own tax number (NIF), so the counts below are distinct entities.

1. **63,209 entities** received corporate income tax (IRC) benefits.

2. **€123,994,570.29** of VAT was refunded in total, spread over 4,318 entities. The largest refund went to **Estado Maior Força Aérea** (Air Force General Staff, NIF 600010686), which received **€37,534,892.43** (IVA!F4309).

3. **About 43.3%** of the VAT refunds went to the armed and security forces category (Forças Armadas e de Segurança). That is €53,648,422.64 out of €123,994,570.29.

4. **414 fire-brigade associations and corps** (Associações e Corpos Bombeiros) received a VAT refund, worth €8,197,951.70 in total.
